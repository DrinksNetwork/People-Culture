Logging a visit is four taps on your phone:

01Open Jobs
Open the Jobs tab and select the job.
02Tap Charges
Select your booking and start the on-job timer at the venue.
03Charge template
Pick Field, Desk, Service or Custom. A standard venue visit is 30 minutes.
04Notes and save
Tag the outcome with quick chips, add real notes, and save.

Your notes are what the brand reads. Quick chips help, but they don’t replace a line about who you spoke to and what happens next.
