01Take the brand on
An opening order of the brand before the night, so the menu can be poured and stays on after.
02Get the mise en place list
The bartender sends the menu and a full list of every spirit, modifier, syrup, garnish and glass at least two weeks before.
03Buy the ingredients
Your venue buys everything on the list, so it's in the building before the guest arrives.
04Run it through the day before
Meet the bartender, check nothing is missing and prep any complicated garnishes.
05Back them on the night
A duty manager and bar back, a station, ice, glassware and POS buttons for the menu.

What you're agreeing to

Hosting a Bar Takeover

  • Lock in a date within 6 weeks, for a night inside 60 days of registering.
  • One guest shift, 6 pm to 10 pm (later if you like), under your licence and house rules.
  • An opening order of the brand before the night, kept on your list after it.
  • Buy the ingredients from the bartender's mise en place list, sent at least two weeks before.
  • A run-through with the bartender the day before, with garnish prep.
  • A duty manager and bar back alongside the guest.
  • Tickets sold through Drinks Network's Eventbrite. Ticket-holder and trade contact details go to the brand that funds the night, and no one else.
