The problem

Prevent fakes of your brand.

SecuriCode uses unique secure codes, real-time tracking and collaboration tools to help consumers and authorities fight illicit alcohol across ASEAN countries.

  • Lack of public awareness

    Consumers often can't verify authenticity, which leaves them distrustful and exposed to dangerous counterfeits.

  • High taxes and prices

    High luxury alcohol taxes encourage smuggling and counterfeiting that bypass regulation.

  • Inadequate enforcement

    Without a way to tell real from fake, authorities struggle to identify and prosecute illicit operators.

  • Lot code tampering

    Falsified or missing tax-tracking codes make dangerous products impossible to trace and recall.

Protect authenticity, build consumer trust and give authorities evidence of counterfeits. Codes start at roughly US$0.05 each.

Why SecuriCode?

Hacked or copied codes get shut down.

  • Unique codes that cannot be faked, unlike QR codes
  • Not a barcode: no two codes are the same
  • Parent and child codes, so every bottle in a missing pallet can be identified
  • A dashboard to track and trace fakes and parallel imports
  • A free app, with codes that stay live at no cost
Know how to spot fake liquor

Can you tell the difference?

Fake, grey and counterfeit liquor is reported to make up 30–40% of sales in ASEAN markets, and methanol poisoning from fakes has caused blindness and deaths. SecuriCode helps brands prevent that lasting damage.
