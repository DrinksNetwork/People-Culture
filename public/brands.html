Brands on RepShare

Distribution hasn't changed in 100 years. Your sales team can.

Running a full-time sales force gets harder every time excise rises. RepShare gives emerging brands seasoned reps — bartenders, brand ambassadors, ex-wholesaler sales — for 5 to 15 hours a week in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane.

They already know the buyers. You get the doors opened, the listings with wholesalers, the in-store tastings and the reorders — with 24/7 results reporting and a 30-day pro-rata money-back guarantee.

Cars, phones, tolls and parking are all included. No salaries. No lock-ins.
