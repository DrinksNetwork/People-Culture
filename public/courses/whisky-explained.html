Whisky Explained

A self-paced introduction to whisky as a craft with a long history, from medieval monks to its place worldwide today. Each module has a short lesson, expert insights and a quiz. For bartenders, retailers and whisky enthusiasts.

What you'll cover
  • History: from medieval monks to global prominence
  • Production: from grain selection to barrel ageing
  • Styles: single malt, blended, Scotch, bourbon, Australian whisky and more
  • Rules and regulations around the world
  • How to drink it: nosing, tasting and serving

Each lesson: review · listen · answer · then unlock the next
