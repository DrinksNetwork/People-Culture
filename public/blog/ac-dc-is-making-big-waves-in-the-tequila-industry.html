AC/DC Shook Tequila All Night Long

By Conor Charlton · 7 September 2024

Legendary rock band AC/DC shocks fans and industry alike with their electrifying new venture into premium tequila, proving they’re still thunderstruck after all these years. Partnering with Amber Beverage Group, they’ve launched Thunderstruck Tequila, proving once again that these rock legends are far from slowing down.

Here is where it becomes intriguing. Along with Amber Beverages Australia, AC/DC has teamed with the tequila producers of Rooster Rojo and Kah tequila to produce Thunderstruck Tequila in a move that would cause even the most hardened rocker to raise an eyebrow. This is a partnership with music and spirit aficionados buzzing.

As I walked into my local bottle store, I saw a familiar sight: the classic AC/DC logo on a sleek tequila bottle. I rubbed my eyes, convinced I’d had one too many, but no, it was real. The most influential rock band in the world had truly gone agave, and I knew I had to look into this strange turn in the AC/DC story.

For those living under a rock (pun intended), AC/DC has been the soundtrack of rebellion since the 1970s. These working-class Aussie men were one of the final holdouts to join Spotify despite smashing out tour after tour. For decades, air guitarists across have used their booming songs “Back in Black” and “Highway to Hell” as their war cry.

My curiosity won out, and I chose to grab a Blanco bottle. Back at home, I poured myself a measure, half expecting it to spring out of the glass and start headbanging. I smelled something really sophisticated—citrus and pepper—that was polished.

Two types of Thunderstruck Tequila are Blanco and Reposado. Perfect for drinking or blending into your own cocktail, the Blanco is a clear, crisp spirit with a strong taste character.

The Reposado provides a deeper, more nuanced flavor and is just long enough to acquire a smooth, somewhat smoky sensation. Two well made choices that will please even the most discriminating palates define a classic example of less is more.

But this tequila is about the experience more than it’s flavor. Drinking Thunderstruck Tequila connects you with the rock ‘n’ roll spirit rather than just consuming. You are drawing on decades of musical history and experiencing the strength of every chord, lyric that has ever characterized AC/DC’s legacy.

The foray of AC/DC into the industry of spirits is not only innovative but also a reflection of a rising trend of booze companies sponsored by celebrities. Stars are using their reputation to upend the business from Ryan Reynolds’ Aviation Gin to George Clooney’s Casamigos. But the genuineness of AC/DC’s Thunderstruck Tequila distinguishes it from other marketing gimmicks; it’s a real expression of the band’s hard-partying attitude.

Though many independent Australian liquor stores offer the brand, the tequila is mostly accessible online. Searching the internet, I came to sites like Barrel and Batch as well as foreign stores like Master of Malt.

It was evident from reading internet reviews that both tequila drinkers and AC/DC diehards were applauding Thunderstruck. One admirer remarked, “It’s like ‘You Shook Me All Night Long’ in a bottle – smooth, potent, and leaves you wanting more.” One of the reluctantly pleased even seasoned spirits experts said, “It’s shockingly well-crafted for a celebrity tequila. AC/DC did their research.

Sitting here drinking my second glass of Thunderstruck Blanco, I find myself marveling at AC/DC’s most recent triumph. As they did the music business all those years ago, they have swept the spirits world. It’s evidence of their timeless appeal and their capacity to surprise us even now, after all these years.

AC/DC has proven time and again that they’re more than just a band – they’re a force of nature. Their exploration of tequila with Thunderstruck marks is another chapter in their legendary history, fusing the rich legacy of Mexican agave with the rebellious attitude of rock ‘n’ roll.

Thunderstruck is worth a try regardless of your level of devotion to the game or merely search for a quality tequila with some edge. Just keep in mind, as with their music, enjoy sensibly; otherwise, you can find yourself on a highway headed toward hangover paradise.
