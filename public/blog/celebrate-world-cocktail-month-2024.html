Celebrate World Cocktail Month 2024

By Hayden Wood · 1 May 2022

If you haven’t already been advised by your social constituents, May is World Cocktail Month. This is the month when the world celebrates the shake, rattle and roll of spirits, tipples and tinctures to form a sum greater than its parts. A time where we raise a glass in celebration of all things luxurious, deluxe and fabulous.

Old-fashioned whiskey sour or a classic whiskey on the rocks may be the taste we still cherish, but the season of Mixology is here to rock your parties and events! Australian wines are a popular option for fine and formal dining, but what if we told you, it is that time of the year when you experience vibrant and colourful drinks and can choose your new favourite this month? Yes, you guessed it correctly: it is World Cocktail Month 2024!

This is where the spirit and cocktail enthusiasts, lovers, and bartenders celebrate the month passionately and create magic with the art of mixing. This month offers a fantastic opportunity for the seasoned mixer, a casual sipper, and experienced and fresher bartenders to give their best shot at creating the most magical and refreshing drink for the audiences. It’s a time of fun, enjoyment, and discovery. Participating lets you enhance your mixing skills, discover new flavours, and connect with a global community of cocktail enthusiasts. So, let’s dive in and understand how we got this opportunity to explore this month, what events we can attend as the bartender or audience, and how this magic happens and discover the new flavours of this season! 

World Cocktail Month

May is World Cocktail Month, so mark your calendars and attend the most happening cocktail events in town. This celebration picked up its pace in the early 2000s when people were still enjoying their classic cocktails during different events, where the bartenders also picked up their skill and experimented with new tastes. Soon, it led to the ear of weekly promotions of one particular flavour for the week, like Martini nights by the hotel, Sangrias by the beach, and so forth. These weekly events soon became popular amongst the audiences, and they started enjoying the fresh tastes and flavours. The glamorous and heroic act of mixing was to be experienced behind the bars by the experienced bartenders. The shakes, rattles and roll of spirits were not part of this month. 

Now, as we know, this has taken over from weekly events to a celebration of the month. This celebration of mixing allowed the freshers to experience and share their charm behind bars and create new magical potions for the audiences. It is time to raise our glasses to the latest flavour and vibrant combination of poison in our parties and events.

Key Milestones in World Cocktail Month

2010s: Formal Recognition

As the weekly event picked up its pace and soon became celebrated across bars during May, by 2010, it received formal recognition as World Cocktail Month. Not only did the food and beverage industry get a new scale to promote their event, but the media also helped promote and started showing the glamorous scale behind bars, as they captured different pictures of bartenders throwing and shaking different mixers in the air to create new magical poison. Bartenders also receive significant recognition for creating unique, vibrant, and excellent mixers for their audiences.

2020s: Global Expansion and Innovation

COVID-19 held the World spread across, but the mixers still enjoyed this event. Well, not in the physical format, but yes, in the Virtual World. As a result, this led to a surge in home bartending and virtual cocktail events, online masterclasses, virtual tastings, and social media challenges that allowed people to celebrate World Cocktail Month from the comfort of their homes, connecting with others worldwide. Still, bartenders created new flavours for people across the country. The craziness for these events has increased since the COVID lockdown lifted, and people have resumed their everyday routines.

Fun Fact The most expensive cocktail ever sold cost a whopping $22,579! This martini, called the "Diamonds Are Forever Martini," included a one-carat diamond alongside vodka and lime juice. World Cocktail Month is a great chance to try something luxurious (for a bit less!)

Notable Collaborations

World Cocktail Month is not just a celebration; it’s a platform for collaboration. Major players in the industry, from marketing companies to exhibition galleries, hotels, stand-alone bars and restaurants, musicians, and media, have joined forces to make this event a huge success. The rise of influencers in social media marketing campaigns has further amplified the reach and impact of these events, making them even more popular and engaging. Sometimes, the event is collaborated on in the bartender’s name alone, as they are famous for creating the best mixers in town.

How to Celebrate World Cocktail Month 2024

Attend a Cocktail Class or Workshop

If you’re eager to learn the art of mixing drinks, now is the perfect time to attend learning sessions. Many hotels, restaurants, and independent bars host such sessions, where you can gain valuable knowledge about spirits, mixing techniques, and flavour combinations. These classes are not only informative but also a fun way to enhance your mixology skills.

Explore New Bars and Cocktails

What could be more thrilling than embarking on a journey to explore new bars and restaurants during this celebratory month? Try their new flavours of the month; you never know what surprising mix and combination will be offered. It’s a chance to step out of your comfort zone and discover unique and exciting cocktails. 

Host a Cocktail Party

If you love playing the host, this is your chance to shine. Invite friends and loved ones who share your passion for Mixology to a cocktail party. You can even hire a bartender or ask your spirit-enthusiast friends to help you create new compositions. With some good music and delicious food, it’s a recipe for a memorable evening of shared enjoyment and discovery.

Participate in Cocktail Challenges

If you are an aspiring bartender and want to present your skills in front of the World, this is your time to shine. Take part in the challenges organized by various organizations and let the media promote you. Learn from others and let them learn from you; socialize and build your contacts here.

Look out for Local Distilleries and Bars

Give your local brands a chance. They can be small or large, but they might allow you to experience something new. Attend events held by these brands and visit their establishments. 

Attend the events and Activities

Look out for the calendars and various events that happen across the cities. If you are an inspiring bartender, these events can be your next gig, so get hired by the professionals. 

  • Tasting events—In addition to cocktail challenges, events, home bartending, and workshops, various tasting sessions and events happen across bars and hotels; this is when you can learn about the spirits and new ones in the market. 
  • Themed Nights – Who doesn’t enjoy a theme-based event, whether we are looking for Bartenders to help us create fantastic theme-based mixers or audiences who like to party with themes? So, here is the opportunity to enjoy it.

Exciting Flavours to Cherish This May 2024

Citrus Glow – It is a vibrant and refreshing cocktail that combines the zestiness of fresh citrus fruits with a hint of herbal complexity. This drink is perfect for those who enjoy a light, invigorating beverage with a twist.

Midnight Ember – It is a dark and sultry cocktail that combines smoky and spicy flavours. It is perfect for an evening drink and features mezcal, a coffee liqueur, and a chilli for a kick.

Tropical Breeze—This bright, fruity cocktail transports you to a tropical paradise. It combines rum with tropical fruits and coconut, offering a sweet and exotic flavour.
Botanical Bliss is a sophisticated cocktail highlighting the delicate flavours of botanical spirits and fresh herbs. It is ideal for those who appreciate floral and herbal notes in their cocktails.

Golden Hour – It is a beautifully layered cocktail that captures the essence of a sunset with its vibrant colours and flavours. This drink combines whiskey with apricot and citrus, creating a warm and inviting beverage.
