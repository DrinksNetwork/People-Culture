What Is Pushing Liquor To Engage Differently

By Hayden Wood · 29 July 2024

The onset of COVID-19 has significantly altered consumer behaviour and how products are sold. Once reliant on physical sales, the liquor industry has transitioned to online sales. This shift has changed the way liquor is sold and the role of storytelling in driving sales, which is now more pronounced in the digital realm.

Storytelling is not just a tool in the liquor industry, it’s a game-changer. It’s the key to creating a brand image that resonates, fostering customer engagement that lasts, and building customer bonds that are unbreakable. In the ever-evolving drinks industry, it’s the strategy that sets brands apart, showing customers just how much they are valued and essential.

Power of Storytelling 

Differentiation 

To keep selling in the market, one must identify the differences and present unique selling points to the customers. The liquor market is highly competitive, and to set your brand apart from the various other options available, storytelling can help you narrate stories about your heritage, production process, or the people behind the brand. These stories can create a unique brand identity and help you stand out in a crowded marketplace. This is the ‘Power of Storytelling-Differentiation’-using storytelling to differentiate your brand and make it more appealing to customers.

Did you know? Sazerac and SouthTrade International wanted to connect with their trade audience. Using facts and education we helped raise the level of whisky awareness and also, by default, raised the value of their brands.

Increased perceived value 

When a brand shares about its heritage, how they create customers’ favourite spirits, or the unique ingredients that go into making a product, it educates customers and makes them believe in the brand even more. This increased belief in the brand’s quality and uniqueness can justify a higher price point, as customers perceive that the spirits they are buying are premium and of the best quality. This is what we mean by ‘increased perceived value’-the customer’s perception of the brand’s worth, which can be enhanced through storytelling. 

Emotional connections

As customers delve into the brand’s culture, heritage, and the unique ingredients that make it stand out, they don’t just learn, they connect. They connect with the brand on an emotional level, feeling a sense of exclusivity that only a compelling story can provide.

Brand loyalty

Storytelling is the best way to get consumers to believe in the brand. As consumers connect emotionally and are ready to pay for their premium spirits, they become loyal customers. The more you connect with your customers and give them attention on a personalised level, the more they will become loyal brand customers.

How to use storytelling in the Australian liquor industry

Audience and create a compelling brand story

Whether you’re in retail or the liquor industry, your audience is the key to creating the most exciting stories. Understanding what they like and what stories they relate to will help you craft narratives that resonate. Market research and a deep understanding of their needs and wants will guide you in creating authentic, relatable stories that align with your brand’s vision and values.

Fun Fact David Vitale from Starward Distillery in Melbourne dives into the reasons why their whisky is fundamentally different from others and the story of how it all came to be. This provides a good narrative for trade and consumers to remember.

Platforms 

If you want to reach your target audience, you must use all the different platforms. Social media, websites, email marketing, and even packaging can all be used to tell the brand’s story. Each platform provides a unique opportunity for them to connect with customers and tell them the company’s story. Our suggestion is, to get good at one thing at a time, focus on one thing at a time.  

 

Engage with your audience

Connecting with and engaging with every customer is essential to making them loyal. One great way to do this is to let loyal customers share their experiences and stories with the brand. These testimonials show your brand’s commitment to customer satisfaction and overall engagement, which would help them convey your brand’s message and stories to new customers. 

 

Obsticle alert. If going it alone in content creation isn't working for your budget, find collaboration and partner events where many can work as one to carry the load. Speed Rack Australia was a Brest Cancer fundraiser using women's speed bartending as the basis for attracting a crowd. The event utilised over 20 brands in partnership.

Visual and interactive elements 

High-quality videos and pictures will help you bring the brand to life, attracting more attention from your target customers. Since all the information is available online, it would be ideal to create virtual tours for clients, letting them experience the world of your distillery and how you produce the finest collection of alcohol and spirits. This way, you can share the brand’s issues with the customers online. Add in the factors of how to address the sustainability practice to reduce the environmental impact, and these points will act as an added advantage to your brand’s commitment and values.

If you have a unique product, help tell the world about it by videoing the story of flavour and usability so YouTube can do the work for you. That’s what Amaro di Angostura di to reach their trade audience.

Measure and adapt

It is essential to look into the factors and how and what storytelling platforms and methods are increasing your customer engagement and conversion numbers. Look into various options that can help you bring in the change to your method of storytelling; for example, hiring an influencer with an exceptional number of followers can help you narrate your story on the social media platform and can help you bring in more sales.

Final Thought –

Storytelling, whether through packaging, social media, advertisements, standing behind the bar, virtual tours online, or even hiring local artists or influencers, can help you communicate the brand’s culture, vision, and values to customers. Customers then connect with the brand emotionally and want to stay connected for more updates; then, they are also ready to pay more for their favourite premium bottle of spirit. As with the above-mentioned fun facts, we can see how many brands have created unique stories to relate to their target customers and helped them develop their brand image.
