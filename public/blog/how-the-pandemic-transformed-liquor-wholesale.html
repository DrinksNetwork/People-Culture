How the Pandemic Transformed Liquor Wholesale

By Hayden Wood · 8 August 2021

Hastened by the global pandemic, online wholesale ordering has quickly become the number one resource for almost everything you can buy in liquor. But where there is digital, we all need a real human.

With the convenience and simplicity of online ordering, it’s no wonder all the major wholesalers now have a marketplace model. The reality is, if you haven’t shifted your business onto digital marketplaces like eBev, ALM or Paramount, you’re missing out on access to a huge potential market.

In

the past, liquor products haven’t generally been the first to turn to for rapid growth online tech. Although many online retailers and shippers have found success using the internet before the pandemic, now there is a seismic shift to online wholesale by ALM, Paramount and other box movers.
