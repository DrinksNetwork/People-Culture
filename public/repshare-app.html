Order servicing

Every order, every channel.

Venues order the way they already buy. Reps raise the order on the spot and RepShare tracks it through to delivery, whichever way it ships.

  • Service orders through ALM, Paramount, ILG or direct from the brand
  • Live status from submitted to confirmed to delivered
  • Reorders logged as service, so new-business time stays on new business
  • Every order tied back to the venue, the brand, the rep and the job

Circuit building

Target the right accounts with the right offer.

Start from 31K+ licensed venues, narrow by suburb and venue type, and build a circuit your reps can actually walk. Attach an offer and every account on the circuit gets it.

  • Filter by city, suburb, venue type and buying status
  • One venue record per site, so circuits never double count
  • Contacts follow the person when they change venues
  • Attach intro deals and high value offers to the accounts that matter
Rep management & order updates

Know what every rep is doing.

Jobs are allocated to reps by brand. Their hours, visits, notes and orders flow back in real time, and anything unusual is held for review before it's charged.

  • Allocate jobs, set bookings and reassign stalled ones
  • On-job timer and quick chips for clean, consistent notes
  • Order updates pushed to the brand the moment they change
  • Approvals for anything over the standard booking

Features & benefits

One app, three sides of the sale.

For brands

  • A fractional sales team without salaries
  • Live dashboard of orders, circuits, products and payments
  • Pay for productive time, checked against the standard
  • Orders serviced through your wholesalers or direct

For reps

  • Every job, booking and brief on your phone
  • On-job timer and quick chips for fast, clean notes
  • Earnings tab, so you always know what you've made
  • Several brands in one visit, charged fairly to each

For venues

  • One rep visit, several great brands
  • Intro deals and offers matched to your venue
  • Order through ALM, Paramount, ILG or direct
  • Tastings and staff training on site
