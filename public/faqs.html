Frequently asked questions

FAQs

Everything people usually ask us. Still stuck? Get in touch.

How does Drinks Network work?

We work with drinks brands, venues and drinks-industry people across production, sales, marketing and hospitality, matching the right people to the right work. For brands, RepShare provides on-demand sales reps.

What types of positions do you specialise in?

Production, sales, marketing, hospitality and retail roles across wine, beer and spirits, plus bottle stores and related businesses.

How can I apply for work?

Start at Start here and choose "I want work", or see current roles on our careers page.

What information do I need to provide?

An up-to-date résumé, your relevant experience, your contact details and anything else the application asks for.

Are there any fees for job seekers?

No. The service is free for job seekers; our partners are the employers and brands we work with.

How long does recruitment usually take?

It depends on the role and the number of applicants. We aim to move quickly and keep you updated along the way.

What makes Drinks Network different?

Deep knowledge of the drinks industry, a team focused on matching talent to roles, and long-term relationships with clients and candidates.

Do you offer career development support?

Yes: guidance on industry trends, skill development and professional growth, plus our courses.

Will my information stay confidential?

Yes. We take data privacy seriously, keep what you submit confidential and use it only for the purpose you gave it. See our privacy policy.

How can I stay up to date on jobs and news?

Subscribe to our newsletter, follow our social channels, or check back here regularly.

Can I contact you for help?

Yes. Book a call or use our contact page.

How can I partner with Drinks Network?

Go to Start here and choose "I want to partner", or contact us and a team member will be in touch.

Where do you operate?

Our main operations are in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane, serving the whole Australian drinks industry. We also place talent in Asia and the UAE.

Do you offer specialised services?

Yes, for employers and job seekers, including tailored recruitment, career guidance and industry insights, plus RepShare and SecuriCode.

How can I give feedback?

Use our contact page. We read everything.
