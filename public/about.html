About us

Good times, people & culture.

A strategic partner for the drinks industry: recruitment, team building, product and brand launches, and content creation.

Since 2012, video and content for major Australian liquor companies.

Now connecting brands, viewers and trade online and offline.

A personal service that complements your people and culture.

Who's behind it?

Hayden Wood

Founder · bartender, author and tech start-up founder

Known as "Woody", Hayden is an internationally recognised bartender, author and drinks creator from New Zealand, now based in Sydney, with more than 25 years in the industry and multiple awards.

He has run over 600 team-building workshops, reached more than 20,000 home cocktail enthusiasts, and his books have won international recognition.

The journey
  1. July 2001

    Hayden Wood publishes his first book, the first of ten on food and beverages.

  2. November 2009

    Contracted to Food Network in New York; four years touring the USA with a food-theatre entertainment show.

  3. May 2012

    Drinks Network is established in Australia, producing video and published work for liquor brands.

  4. March 2020

    Shifts to a retail and events marketplace as online sales grow.

  5. January 2021

    Launches as an online marketplace.

  6. June 2023

    Winds up the eCommerce business.

  7. November 2023

    Drinks Network 2.0 launches to meet demand for drinks industry staff, leading to RepShare.
