The retail challenge

Winning the shelf is half the job. Holding it is the other half.

  • National ranging is won at head office but lost in store: facings slip, stock runs out, and nobody is there to see it.
  • Traditional agencies sell in, but few service every store, every month, with proof it happened.
  • Tastings drive rate of sale, but they need reliable weekend execution, not one-off promo staff.

Retail ranging decisions are driven by movement and reliability, not intent. This is a service program, not another sales agency.

How the program runs
  1. Build your store circuit: the right stores, the right offer, the right reps.
  2. A dedicated brand manager plans the month and owns the result.
  3. Monthly store servicing: every store visited, every month, no exceptions.
  4. Weekend tastings where they move the most product.
  5. Photo proof from every visit: you see the shelf as the shopper sees it.
  6. Orders written on the spot, with gaps and out-of-stocks flagged the same day.
  7. Live dashboard reporting: every visit, order and tasting in your RepShare account.
