Part A

The host venue

The home of the night and the major contact for the event. Your venue, logo and photos go front and centre on the ticket page, social posts and paid ads.

The host venue provides

  • A major contact for the event, before and on the night
  • An opening order of the tasted range at wholesale, on a mixed-case intro deal
  • The space, glassware, water and bar staff
  • Food: snacks, or a higher food-inclusive ticket
  • Date, start time and guest capacity
  • Venue logo and photos for the ticket page
  • Promotion to its own guests and socials

The host venue receives

  • 50% of tasting-ticket revenue after Eventbrite fees, paid in cash with a sales statement
  • A higher share on food-inclusive tickets
  • A mixed-case intro deal at wholesale on the brand's range
  • A ticketed crowd from the Drinks Network campaign
  • Ticket-holder and trade attendee lists
  • Event artwork, and no ticketing admin

Glassware hire, if required, is usually split 50/50 between the host venue and the Drinks Network / brand side.

Part of the deal

Stock what you pour

Hosting means becoming a patron of the brand. The host venue buys a set of the products tasted on the night, so guests can keep ordering them long after the event.

  • Wholesale pricing on the tasted range
  • A mixed-case intro deal: a good offer to get your foot in the door with the brand
  • Ordered through Drinks Network before the event, so it's on your shelf on the night

Bring your own people

  • Venue staff join free.
  • Your trade guests book a trade ticket.
  • Friends and regulars book a paying ticket.
  • Every invited guest is either trade or paying. One or the other.
